Understanding the Metrics

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)

The gross revenue generated for every rupee spent on advertising. A ROAS of 3.0x means you earned ₹3 for every ₹1 spent. Crucial for measuring direct profitability.

CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)

The total cost to acquire one paying customer. Derived by dividing Total Spend by Total Purchases. Compare this against your Average Order Value (AOV) to ensure margins remain healthy.

CTR (Click-Through Rate)

The percentage of users who clicked your ad after seeing it. A high CTR indicates strong creative resonance and audience alignment. Typically, > 1.5% is considered healthy.

CPC & CPM

CPC (Cost Per Click): Price paid for each link click.
CPM (Cost Per Mille): Price to show the ad 1,000 times. High CPMs indicate high auction competition.
